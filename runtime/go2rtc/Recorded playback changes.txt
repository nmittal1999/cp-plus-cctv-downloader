This app uses a build of go2rtc v1.9.14 with a small recorded-playback extension:
- Conn.Scale in pkg/rtsp/conn.go
- Optional scale fragment parameter in internal/rtsp/rtsp.go
- Optional Scale header in the RTSP PLAY request in pkg/rtsp/client.go

Normal 1x playback sends no Scale header, as in upstream go2rtc.
The app supports 0.5x, 1x, 2x, 4x and 8x. The recorder must honor RTSP Scale.
No video transcoding is added.

The bundled browser player fixes playbackRate to the selected rate instead
of upstream live-stream latency catch-up. It reports the video position to
our UI so jump controls can reopen the recorder at the requested timestamp.

The build script produces go2rtc-speed.exe from upstream v1.9.14 and our patch.
It is covered by the MIT license in LICENSE.txt.

Timing fix: pkg/mp4/muxer.go preserves video frame gaps up to 60 seconds,
so keyframes from fast recorded replay retain the recorded timeline.
Zero timestamps and invalid large jumps retain the original protection.
Browser input buffer grows as needed instead of failing at 2 MiB.
Startup buffering has a one-second fallback once a decoded frame exists.
2x browser playback confirmed by the user after the timing fix.
